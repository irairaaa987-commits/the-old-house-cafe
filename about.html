THE OLD HOUSE, MYSURU

Where heritage meets
everyday life.

The Old House is a distinctive café and dining space in Mysuru, created within a traditional family villa that is over a century old.

Instead of replacing the character of the old property, the space embraces its history and transforms it into a warm, green sanctuary where people can gather and enjoy time together.

OUR IDENTITY

Simple design.
Timeless character.

The Old House brings together an elegant, minimalist identity with the rustic charm of a European-style villa.

High wooden ceilings, warm lanterns, greenery and quiet garden corners create an atmosphere that feels relaxed and unhurried.

HOW IT BEGAN

From a family home
to a gathering place.

What stands today on JLB Road near the RTO Circle was originally built more than a century ago as a quiet family residence.

The owners chose to retain the character of the ancestral structure, preserving elements such as its columns, brickwork and old-world architectural character.

The space was first imagined as Maya Bistro before developing into the distinctive pizzeria and gelateria known as The Old House.

OUR PHILOSOPHY

No rush.
No unnecessary fuss.
Just enjoy the moment.

The Old House is designed around a relaxed way of experiencing a café — taking your time, enjoying conversations and feeling comfortable in the surroundings.

The emphasis is on wholesome vegetarian offerings, carefully chosen ingredients and an environment where families and friends can spend meaningful time together.

MORE THAN A CAFÉ

A space built around
the experience.

The Old House brings several experiences together under one roof, combining the atmosphere of an old Mysuru home with European-inspired café traditions.

Its identity includes a professional pizzeria, an artisanal gelateria and an in-house patisserie, all within the character of the old property.

The result is a place where the setting and the café experience feel naturally connected.
